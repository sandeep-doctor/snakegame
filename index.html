HOW TO PLAY

  • Move — Arrow keys / WASD, swipe on the board, or the D-pad
  • Pause — P, Space or Esc
  • Music — M  ·  Sound FX — N
  • Chain fruits quickly to build a combo multiplier (up to x5)
  • Fruits are worth different points: 🍎 10 · 🍊 15 · 🫐 20 · 🍇 25 · 🍒 30 · ✨ golden 50
  • Golden stars appear every 5 fruits — grab them before they vanish for big points
  • Glowing orbs grant power-ups: Slow-Mo, 2X Points, Shield (10s, protects walls, obstacles + self-bites, flashes last 3s) and Magnet
  • Every 5 fruits you level up: faster snake, more obstacles
  • Wrap-around mode turns walls into portals — the snake dives through instead of dying
  • Unlock new snake colors by beating score milestones — or buy them in the Store
